# SplitSmart Clarification Questions

These questions are to be asked during `/speckit-clarify`. Do not treat any answer as decided until the customer/instructor confirms it.

1. Must the payer also be one of the expense participants?
2. Who may invite, add, remove, or leave a group? What happens to prior records when someone leaves?
3. How should leftover cents be assigned for equal splits, and what rounding rule should percentage splits use?
4. Which expense amounts are invalid (for example, zero or negative values), and which currency/currencies are supported?
5. Must percentage shares add to exactly 100%, and what should happen if they do not?
6. What login, account creation, and password recovery behavior is required?
7. Who receives expense notifications, through what channel, and can notifications be disabled?
8. What details must a payment record include, and should the app suggest a simplified settlement plan?
9. Who can approve or reject an expense? Can its creator approve it? Is one approval sufficient?
10. Which report filters, parameters, and export options are required?
11. What receipt image formats and size limits are allowed, and who can access receipts?
12. Is the Java Swing MVP only a course subset of the larger web/Android/iOS product?
